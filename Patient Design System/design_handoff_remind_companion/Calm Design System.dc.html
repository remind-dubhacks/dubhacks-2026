Design system

Calm

A companion screen for people living with dementia. It shows a soft sky-to-meadow gradient, and sometimes a photo or a few words. Nothing else.

The three elements

Ground
Vertical gradient, always on.
Top rgb(214,232,240)
Bottom rgb(230,239,216)
Aa
Words
Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, in ink #1E2B26. Made for low vision: open shapes, no confusable letters. Contrast 11.7:1 at the top, 12.4:1 at the bottom.
Photos
One at a time, centered. Soft 32px corners and a soft shadow. No frames, captions overlays or controls.

Type

Message · 64 / 80 · Semibold
Good morning, Margaret.
Caption · 40 / 54 · Regular
This is you and Tom at the lake.

Screens

Rest. The default. Most of the day looks like this.
Good morning, Margaret.
Message. One short sentence, centered. Twelve words at most.
Photo. A familiar face or place, on its own.
This is you and Tom at the lake.
Photo with caption. Name the people. Say it as a friend would.

Rules

  1. Only the gradient, a photo, and words. No buttons, icons, clocks or badges.
  2. One thing on screen at a time.
  3. Change slowly. Fade in and out over 1.2 seconds; never slide or flash.
  4. Write warmly, in the present tense, using names. Never test or correct.
  5. Text is always ink on the gradient. Never place text over a photo.
