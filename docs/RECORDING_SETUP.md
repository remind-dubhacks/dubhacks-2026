# Shuffle recording: setup card

Print this out and keep it next to the arms. The Record screen (`/studio/record`) shows the same steps live.

## The row: 4 slots in one line, nothing in front

| Slot 1 (left) | Slot 2 | Slot 3 | Slot 4 (right end) |
|---|---|---|---|
| ⚫ **black keycap** keyring | ⚪ **white keycap** keyring | 🔵 **blue Salesforce** keyring | *empty* (parking spot) |

- Tape the 4 slots in a straight line, evenly spaced, in reach of the follower.
- **This is the START position**, for every episode and after every reset.
- Slot 4 is only a parking spot. Every shuffle ends with it **empty** again.

## The 5 shuffles

You move the **leader** arm and the follower copies you. Do the moves in this order, slowly, then rest the arm.

| # | Shuffle (what the model is told) | Moves, in order | END position (slot 1 · 2 · 3, slot 4 empty) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | swap the left and right objects | black keycap → slot 4 · blue Salesforce → slot 1 · black keycap → slot 3 | 🔵 blue · ⚪ white · ⚫ black |
| 2 | swap the left and middle objects | black keycap → slot 4 · white keycap → slot 1 · black keycap → slot 2 | ⚪ white · ⚫ black · 🔵 blue |
| 3 | swap the middle and right objects | white keycap → slot 4 · blue Salesforce → slot 2 · white keycap → slot 3 | ⚫ black · 🔵 blue · ⚪ white |
| 4 | rotate the objects one spot to the left | black keycap → slot 4 · white keycap → slot 1 · blue Salesforce → slot 2 · black keycap → slot 3 | ⚪ white · 🔵 blue · ⚫ black |
| 5 | rotate the objects one spot to the right | blue Salesforce → slot 4 · white keycap → slot 3 · black keycap → slot 2 · blue Salesforce → slot 1 | 🔵 blue · ⚫ black · ⚪ white |

Each shuffle is recorded **12 times**, 60 episodes in total, in batches of 4 that rotate through the 5 shuffles.

## Each episode (the Mac voice tells you)

1. **"Episode N of 4. Go."** You have 50 seconds. Do the moves in the table, then rest the arm.
2. **"Five seconds."** Finish up.
3. **"Reset the objects."** You have 15 seconds. Put the keyrings back in the **START** position by hand, with slot 4 empty.
4. **"Get ready."** The next episode starts.

After 4 episodes there's a 15-second break. The voice then announces the next shuffle; set the START position and press **Ready**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Bad move, or anything unsafe:** press **Stop** on the Record screen or the robot's power switch. The episode in progress is discarded.
- **Gripper fault:** press **Clear gripper fault**, then **Start / Resume**. Grip **lightly**: close the leader trigger only until the follower holds the keyring.
- **A joint won't clear:** unplug the **follower's** round DC power plug for 10 seconds while supporting the arm, then plug it back in.
